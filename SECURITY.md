# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you discover a vulnerability in any Credit Cooperative contract, app, or service, **do not open a public issue**.
Email **contact@creditcoop.xyz** with:

- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- The affected repository and commit, or the on-chain address(es) and chain
- Your contact details for follow-up

We aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours and will coordinate disclosure with you.

Repositories with their own `SECURITY.md` (for example, scope rules for community modules) take precedence over this policy.
