# Security Policy

Credit Cooperative takes the security of its contracts, apps and services seriously. Thank you for helping keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

> **Please do not open a public issue.**
> Email **[security@creditcoop.xyz](mailto:security@creditcoop.xyz)** instead.

### What to include

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | What the vulnerability is and what impact it has |
| **Reproduction** | Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept |
| **Location** | The affected repository and commit, or the on-chain address(es) and chain |
| **Contact** | How we can reach you for follow-up |

### What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge reports within **72 hours**.
- We will keep you informed as we investigate.
- We will coordinate disclosure with you.

## Scope

This policy applies to every repository in the `credit-cooperative` organisation.
Repositories with their own `SECURITY.md` (for example, scope rules for community modules) take precedence over this policy.
